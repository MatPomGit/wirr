# Third-party texture assets

The optional material-learning prefabs and HDRI environments use a curated subset of assets whose identifiers correspond to ambientCG materials.

## Surface material families

- AcousticFoam003
- Concrete032
- DiamondPlate005D
- Fabric023
- Fabric066
- Metal004
- Metal044A
- PaintedWood007A
- Sign002
- Grass001
- WoodFloor034
- WoodFloor040

## HDRI environment families

- DayEnvironmentHDRI101 — Amsterdam
- DaySkyHDRI006B — Clean Horizon
- DaySkyHDRI046A — Day Sky
- EveningEnvironmentHDRI003 — Evening Environment
- DayEnvironmentHDRI078 — Forrest
- IndoorEnvironmentHDRI012 — Indoor Environment
- DayEnvironmentHDRI096 — Near Lake
- NightSkyHDRI008 — Night Sky
- DaySkyHDRI039A — Tower

The HDRI folders contain high-dynamic-range EXR files used by the WiRR skybox helper and tone-mapped JPG previews.

ambientCG publishes its assets under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal license.

Source and license information:
https://ambientcg.com/

These asset files are kept separate from the MIT-licensed WiRR source code in `Textures/Surfaces/` and `Textures/HDRI/`.
